# TO-04A — Cross-Market Comparison

**Research snapshot:** 2026-11-24  
**Status:** desk research only; no candidate is validated or approved

## Summary

| Candidate | What desk research supports | Main weakness / unknown | Current disposition |
|---|---|---|---|
| Tour operations | Local marine safety context; established booking and resource-management software | No verified local operator workflow, recordkeeping gap, costly incident, or willingness to pay | Retain one narrow falsification hypothesis only |
| Rental housing | Established portals, agent verification, brokerage and landlord-paid commission models; scoped TM30 compliance lead | No demonstrated underserved renter/landlord problem or defensible wedge | Stop generic portal; compliance is secondary research only |
| Travel/activity discovery | Klook has a broad Pattaya catalogue; GetYourGuide has supplier and reseller/affiliate routes | No demonstrated traveller pain or credible low-cost distribution advantage | Stop broad aggregator / intermediary |

## Scores from the initial screening

The initial screening notes recorded the following totals:

- Tour operations: **24/50**
- Rental housing: **22/50**
- Travel and activity discovery: **18/50**

These are rough desk-research screening scores, not market measurements, statistical estimates, or a validated ranking. They reflect relative ease of identifying a cheap next falsification step, not proven commercial attractiveness. The small differences should not be interpreted as meaningful evidence of market quality.

In particular, Tour Operations' higher score does **not** mean it is the best business opportunity. It is only the candidate with the most concrete, low-cost next question identified in this research pass.

## Decision

1. Do not build a new product or vertical implementation from TO-04A.
2. Do not pivot TourOps into rental housing or travel aggregation.
3. Keep the existing TourOps demo as a synthetic workflow; it is not evidence of customer demand.
4. If the founder later authorizes it, consider one consented observation and record review with a marine operator to test the narrow per-departure evidence hypothesis.
5. Keep marine and land-based activity businesses separate. A friend's farm / venue is not evidence for marine safety compliance.
6. If the operator's existing process is adequate or no costly gap is found, stop this hypothesis too.

## What would change the decision

A candidate should advance only when evidence identifies:
- a specific user and buyer;
- a recent, repeated, consequential problem;
- the actual current workaround and why it fails;
- a reachable first customer segment;
- a plausible lawful operating model;
- a meaningful behavioural signal or commercial intent;
- economics that can work after acquisition, service, support, refunds, and operational burden.

## Evidence boundaries

This comparison relies on public vendor pages, a local news report, and a secondary immigration alert. It does not contain primary statutory analysis, customer interviews, workflow observations, transaction data, paid demand, or independent market sizing.

No warm access path mentioned by the founder is treated as consent, partnership, validation, or a committed pilot. No outreach, sales call, public post, data collection, or production test was performed.
