# TO-04A — Evidence-Led Opportunity Discovery

**Research snapshot:** 2026-11-24  
**Status:** desk research only; no market validation  
**Cash spent:** $0  
**Product decision:** none  
**Next action:** no outreach or pilot is authorized by this document.

## Purpose

Compare three candidate problem spaces without treating the existing TourOps prototype as the predetermined answer:

1. Tour operations in the Pattaya / Na Jomtien / Ocean Marina / Sattahip / Rayong area.
2. Rental-housing discovery and transaction support in Thailand.
3. Travel and activity discovery / distribution in Pattaya.

This is an evidence review, not a market-validation report. Public sources can establish that regulations, products, listings, and commercial models exist. They cannot establish that a particular local operator experiences costly pain, wants a new tool, or will pay for one.

## Executive conclusion

- **Tour operations:** retain one narrow hypothesis for possible falsification: whether marine operators have a recurring, costly gap in per-departure safety / coordination evidence. This is not a discovered niche. The public safety report does not establish a digital recordkeeping obligation or operator pain.
- **Rental housing:** do not pursue a generic listing or discovery portal on current evidence. Incumbents already provide search, agent verification, viewing coordination, and success-based brokerage. Landlord-side compliance is only a separate research lead, not a validated opportunity.
- **Travel and activities:** do not pursue a new broad aggregator or intermediary on current evidence. Klook already presents a substantial Pattaya catalogue, while GetYourGuide has distinct direct-supplier and reseller/affiliate routes.

No candidate currently has demonstrated repeated pain, behavioural evidence, commercial intent, willingness to pay, or validated unit economics.

## Evidence discipline

Use the repository's [Opportunity Discovery Framework](../opportunity-discovery-framework.md). In particular:

- Desk research is not customer evidence.
- A warm introduction is an access path, not a conversation or partnership.
- A regulatory requirement does not automatically create a software need.
- A competitor's feature list does not prove that customers are dissatisfied with it.
- Vendor-reported metrics and pricing are vendor claims, not independent market sizing.
- Dynamic marketplace counts and availability must be date-stamped.
- Do not transfer marine-specific requirements to land-based activity businesses.

## Founder constraints and permissions

This research was conducted without spending money, contacting prospects, publishing publicly, collecting customer data, or running a pilot. Those activities remain out of scope unless explicitly authorized by the founder.

Potential warm access paths mentioned by the founder are not treated as consent, confirmed partnerships, or validated demand. In particular, a land-based farm / activity venue is not evidence for a marine compliance workflow.
