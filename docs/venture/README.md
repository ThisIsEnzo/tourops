# Adaptable Venture Foundation

**Status:** proposed operating model; no new product or market is approved.  
**Last reviewed:** 2026-11-24  
**Cash budget:** $0 unless explicitly changed by the founder.

## Purpose

This repository is evolving from a single-niche TourOps concept workspace into a lightweight foundation for discovering, validating, and launching focused digital products.

The goal is not to build a universal SaaS platform or a venture-studio application. The goal is to create a repeatable way to:

1. identify expensive, recurring problems in real markets;
2. understand current workarounds and incumbent alternatives;
3. test whether a specific group will adopt and pay for a better solution;
4. choose the smallest viable product that addresses the proven problem;
5. reuse only the technology and operating capabilities that genuinely transfer.

TourOps remains one candidate product hypothesis. It is not the mandatory destination of this work.

## Operating principles

- **Problem before product.** Do not select a niche because it is convenient, familiar, or technically interesting.
- **Evidence before commitment.** Separate observations, assumptions, access, conversations, intent, and payment.
- **Manual before automation.** Use existing tools and concierge workflows to test demand before building infrastructure.
- **Narrow before horizontal.** Start with one customer segment and one high-value workflow.
- **Reusable foundations, separate domains.** Reuse proven technical capabilities; do not force unrelated business logic into a common domain model.
- **Reversible decisions.** Keep experiments cheap and easy to stop.
- **Founder constraints are real.** Current default: $0 spend, no cold outreach, no sales calls, no public publishing, and no real customer data without explicit authorization.
- **No progress theatre.** Documents, prototypes, traffic, and access paths are not customer validation.
