# Venture Foundation Decision Log

## 2026-11-24 — Reframe the workspace

**Decision:** Treat TourOps as the first market experiment, not as a permanent commitment to tourism software.

**Why:** The founder wants to identify a strong, defensible problem before committing to a specific market. Potential areas include tour operations, travel/activity discovery, and rental housing.

**Implications:**
- Add a cross-market opportunity discovery process.
- Keep existing TourOps validation work and demo isolated.
- Do not rename the repository or product yet.
- Do not build a universal SaaS platform.
- Do not start a new vertical implementation until its problem and commercial path are supported by evidence.

## 2026-11-24 — Reusable foundation strategy

**Decision:** Reuse capabilities only after repeated needs are demonstrated across concrete products.

**Why:** Premature generalization would add complexity without proven value.

**Implications:**
- Keep shared capabilities separate from product-specific domain logic.
- Prototype manually before building infrastructure.
- Prefer reversible, low-cost experiments.
- Treat privacy, data rights, trust, and operational fulfilment as product requirements.

## 2026-11-24 — TO-04A evidence-led opportunity discovery

**Decision:** Do not approve a market pivot or product build from desk research. Retain one narrow marine-operations hypothesis for possible falsification only.

**Research snapshot:** Three candidate spaces were reviewed: tour operations, rental housing, and travel/activity discovery. The review is desk research only; no customer conversations, workflow observations, behavioural evidence, commercial intent, or paid use were obtained.

**Findings and boundaries:**
- Tour operations: local marine safety measures are documented in public reporting, but that does not establish a digital/written recordkeeping obligation or an operator pain point. Per-departure evidence/coordination remains a hypothesis.
- Rental housing: generic listing discovery is unattractive against established portals and brokerage services. Landlord-side TM30 compliance is a secondary lead only; the cited BOI Single Window long-stay visa scope must not be generalized to all visa applications.
- Travel/activity discovery: a broad new aggregator is unattractive against established catalogues. GetYourGuide's restriction on resellers applies to direct supply-partner onboarding; separate reseller/affiliate routes exist.
- Marketplace counts, vendor pricing, and vendor performance claims are dynamic or self-reported and must be treated accordingly.
- Marine compliance research must not be transferred to land-based venues or activities.

**Next step:** No outreach or pilot is authorized. If separately approved by the founder, one consented observation and record review with a marine operator may test whether a recent, consequential per-departure evidence gap exists. If the current process is adequate or no material pain is found, stop the hypothesis.

**Evidence status:** desk research only. Warm access paths are not conversations, consent, partnerships, or validation. Existing TourOps demo data remains synthetic.
