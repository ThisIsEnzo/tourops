# Venture Foundation Decision Log

## 2026-11-24 — Reframe the workspace

**Decision:** Treat TourOps as the first market experiment, not as a permanent commitment to tourism software.

**Why:** The founder wants to identify a strong, defensible problem before committing to a specific market. Potential areas include tour operations, travel/activity discovery, and rental housing.

**Implications:**
- Add a cross-market opportunity discovery process.
- Keep existing TourOps validation work and demo isolated.
- Do not rename the repository or product yet.
- Do not build a universal SaaS platform.
- Do not start a new vertical implementation until its problem and commercial path are supported by evidence.

## 2026-11-24 — Reusable foundation strategy

**Decision:** Reuse capabilities only after repeated needs are demonstrated across concrete products.

**Why:** Premature generalization would add complexity without proven value.

**Implications:**
- Keep shared capabilities separate from product-specific domain logic.
- Prototype manually before building infrastructure.
- Prefer reversible, low-cost experiments.
- Treat privacy, data rights, trust, and operational fulfilment as product requirements.

## Open questions

- Which market has the strongest combination of pain, reachability, willingness to pay, and viable economics?
- What is the smallest test that can compare tourism and rental-housing problems?
- Which capabilities, if any, are genuinely common across the first two product candidates?
- What go-to-market work is the founder willing to do, and what requires a commercial partner?
