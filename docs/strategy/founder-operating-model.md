# Founder Operating Model — Technical Founder

**Decision date:** 2026-11-16  
**Cash budget for initial validation:** $0.

## Role

The founder chooses to operate as the **technical founder**, not the salesperson.

Founder responsibilities:
- Technical architecture and implementation.
- Technical feasibility, security and quality decisions.
- Review product requirements and demo behavior.
- Decide whether evidence justifies building a narrow MVP.
- Approve any future spending, hiring, partnerships or change in role.

Explicitly out of scope in the current phase:
- Cold calling or cold emailing.
- Sales meetings, negotiations or persuasion.
- Repeated follow-ups with prospects.
- Paid advertising, lead-generation agencies or sales contractors.
- Day-to-day account management.

## Assistant responsibilities

ChatGPT can research public market and competitor information; identify candidate segments and publicly visible prospects; prepare strategy, requirements, pricing hypotheses and validation materials; design synthetic demo scenarios; maintain documentation; and analyze feedback supplied to it.

ChatGPT must not claim to have contacted a company, interviewed an operator, published content or received a customer signal unless the event actually occurred and was verified.

## No-budget rule

Allowed:
- Existing computer, software and already available accounts.
- Public websites, documentation, public reviews and free research sources.
- Static pages on a genuinely free hosting tier, subject to current terms.
- Synthetic data and a clickable demo.
- Organic inbound content prepared for publication.
- Self-serve feedback using a free form or direct email link.

Not allowed without new approval:
- Subscriptions, paid API usage, ads, lead lists or paid research.
- Contractors or agencies.
- Paid integrations or cloud upgrades.
- Real customer/passenger information in a concept demo.

A free tier may have usage limits or commercial restrictions. Check current terms before deploying.

## Human involvement boundary

Potential operators should be able to understand the concept, inspect a synthetic demo, provide optional structured feedback asynchronously, and request more information without pressure.

If a serious inbound prospect asks to speak with someone, do not pretend a sales function exists. The founder can answer a technical question, defer the conversation, or later authorize a revenue-share business-development partner.

## Future sales capacity — only after a demand signal

Possible later options:
1. A commission/revenue-share business-development partner with written scope and ethical sales practices.
2. A part-time independent sales operator paid from collected revenue, not speculative future revenue.
3. Founder-led technical/product demo only, while another person handles commercial qualification and follow-up.

Do not recruit a co-founder or give away equity merely to avoid early conversations. First prove the problem and define the work that needs ownership.

## Decision rights

The founder retains final approval over product scope, architecture, any paid commitment, data handling, customer terms, sales partnerships and compensation. The assistant may recommend; it does not spend money, sign contracts or make commitments on behalf of TourOps.
