# TourOps Validation Reset

**Decision date:** 2026-11-24  
**Purpose:** deliberately slow the project down and prioritize evidence over shipping features.

## Current state

- A polished, interactive synthetic demo exists.
- TO-01D quality/architecture work is recorded as merged in the project history.
- No live customers, discovery conversations, pilots, payments, or validated operator pain are confirmed in the current baseline.
- A list of research candidates and founder-reported warm access paths exists; an access path is not engagement.
- The demo is not production-ready booking/operations software.

Re-check these statements before using them as public claims. The [validation scorecard](./validation-scorecard.md) is the current activity summary.

## Why we are slowing down

Building features is controllable and gives visible progress. Market evidence is slower and uncertain. This creates a risk of polishing a product that does not solve a high-priority problem. From now on, feature count, roadmap completion, and architecture work are not the primary progress measures.

## Operating rules

1. **Freeze features.** No new feature, backend, infrastructure, or extra demo polish by default.
2. **One step at a time.** Complete one discovery action and review it before deciding the next.
3. **No artificial deadlines or sample quotas.** Targets may guide effort but cannot force evidence or imply statistical confidence.
4. **Evidence beats compliments.** Ask for recent examples, frequency, impact, and current workaround.
5. **No automatic green gates.** A threshold can trigger discussion, not approve a build.
6. **Respect the founder's constraints.** No cold outreach, unsolicited messages, sales calls, spend, or public publishing without explicit authorization.
7. **No private data.** Keep notes anonymized and avoid passenger/customer/employee/payment details.
8. **Separate access, conversation, problem, replication, evaluation, and commercial evidence.**
9. **Stop honestly.** Access blocked, contradictory evidence, or no meaningful pain are valid outcomes.

## The single next step

Review the [warm-access map](./warm-market-access-map.md) and decide whether one natural introduction is comfortable. If yes, have one short exploratory conversation without pitching the demo. If no, document the constraint and pause—do not compensate by building more.

## Prototype approval requires a fresh decision

A prototype is not approved by time elapsed or checklist completion. Before proposing one, show the actual evidence: independent examples, frequency, consequences, current workaround, target user/buyer, and an explicit evaluation opportunity. Then make a deliberate continue-discovery / prototype-discussion / pause decision.

Use the [validation workspace](../validation/README.md) to record evidence and contradictions. A prototype discussion is not implementation authorization.

## What success looks like right now

A better understanding of the problem—or a clear reason to stop—is progress. Another feature is not.
