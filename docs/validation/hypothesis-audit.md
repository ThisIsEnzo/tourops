# Hypothesis Audit — TourOps Demo and Product Thesis

**Basis:** product/strategy documents currently in the repository, reviewed 2026-11-24.  
**Stance:** skeptical. The demo is a discussion aid, not proof of demand.

## What the current demo proves

The repository describes a synthetic marine-operator workflow covering enquiry/quote, booking, deposit status, resource assignment, and departure preparation. This demonstrates that a concept workflow can be represented in software. It does not show that operators experience these stages, need one system for them, will adopt it, or will pay.

## Unverified assumptions

1. Operational failures are frequent and costly enough to motivate change (H1).
2. A reachable operator segment owns the workflow and can adopt a solution (H2).
3. Bookings commonly begin with an enquiry and custom quote (H3).
4. Resource assignment or departure readiness is a recurring software-solvable problem (H4).
5. Deposit tracking adds value beyond bank apps, chat, and marketplace processes (H5).
6. A new tool can avoid costly duplicate entry and be maintained by the real daily user (H6).
7. The daily user can use the proposed device, language, and interface (H7).
8. A buyer has a credible economic reason and ability to pay (H8).

All remain UNKNOWN until supported by dated evidence.

## Main risks in the current workflow

- **Too many stages:** each stage can create data-entry and maintenance burden. Every stage must earn its place.
- **Enquiry-first may be wrong:** marketplace, agent, repeat, fixed-price, and walk-in bookings may arrive already confirmed.
- **Deposit tracking may be commoditized:** bank app + transfer proof + chat may be sufficient for some operators.
- **Assignment value may vary by size:** a solo owner with one boat may coordinate verbally; pain may appear only with multiple vessels, crew, or subcontractors.
- **Readiness checklists can create liability expectations:** do not make safety-critical claims or position software as a substitute for required procedures.
- **The incumbent is often the free stack:** LINE or other chat, notebook, spreadsheet, calendar, and bank app. A clean UI alone is not a reason to switch.
- **Adoption can fail despite interest:** the owner may like a demo while staff must do duplicate entry or cannot use the interface.

## What would invalidate or reshape the direction

- Operators cannot describe recent, consequential incidents.
- The booking flow is owned by marketplaces/agents rather than the operator.
- Existing tools already handle the relevant problem adequately.
- The daily user cannot maintain a separate system.
- Problems differ so much between operators that no narrow repeatable workflow emerges.
- There is no plausible commercial path within the founder's chosen operating constraints.

## Audit conclusion

TourOps remains an unvalidated product hypothesis. The correct next move is not to complete the demo backlog or build infrastructure. It is to use one permitted discovery opportunity, if the founder chooses, then review what was learned before taking another step.

This audit must be updated when evidence changes. It must not be used as a substitute for the evidence register.
