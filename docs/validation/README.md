# Validation Workspace

**Status:** active discovery framework; no market validation claimed.  
**Last reviewed:** 2026-11-24  
**Cash budget:** $0 by default.

This workspace supports evidence-led decisions about the current TourOps hypothesis. It does not approve a product build or establish that marine operators have a problem.

## Sources of truth

- [Market evidence register](./market-evidence-register.md) — dated evidence and unknowns.
- [Product hypotheses](./product-hypotheses.md) — claims that evidence may support or disconfirm.
- [Operator walkthrough](./operator-walkthrough.md) — optional guide for a warm, consented conversation.
- [Operator feedback template](./operator-feedback-template.md) — anonymized session notes.
- [Validation decision framework](./validation-decision-framework.md) — how to interpret evidence and choose the next step.
- [Hypothesis audit](./hypothesis-audit.md) — skeptical review of the current demo and product thesis.

The [strategy validation scorecard](../strategy/validation-scorecard.md) remains the single summary of confirmed activity. Do not maintain a competing scorecard here.

## Operating rules

- One discovery action at a time; review evidence before deciding what follows.
- No artificial deadlines, interview quotas, or automatic green gates.
- No cold outreach, unsolicited messages, sales calls, public publishing, or spending without explicit authorization.
- Warm access is only a possible route; it is not consent, engagement, or validation.
- Desk research creates context and hypotheses, not proof of internal workflows or willingness to pay.
- Keep all customer, passenger, employee, payment, and confidential business data out of this repository.
- Synthetic Siam Blue Charters data is demonstration material, never market evidence.
- Feature work remains frozen by default. Evidence does not automatically authorize a prototype.
